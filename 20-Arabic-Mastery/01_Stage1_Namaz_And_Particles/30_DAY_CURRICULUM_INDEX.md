## 📅 WEEK 1: The Core Building Blocks & Daily Prayer (Days 1 – 7)

### Day 1: What is a Word? (The 3 Universal Categories)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: In Arabic, every single word you speak or recite is either:
  1. **اِسْم (Ism)** = A Name or Thing (No clock attached). Examples: *Kitab* (book), *Makkah*, *Zayd*.
  2. **فِعْل (Fi'l)** = An Action with a clock/tense. Examples: *Khalaqa* (He created), *Ya'lamu* (He knows).
  3. **حَرْف (Harf)** = A small connector word. Examples: *Fee* (in), *Min* (from), *Wa* (and).
* **Today's 3 Words**:
  1. **كِتَابٌ** (*Kitaabun*) = Book (Ism) [Urdu: کتاب].
  2. **ذَهَبَ** (*Dhahaba*) = He went (Fi'l).
  3. **فِي** (*Fee*) = In / Inside (Harf) [Urdu: میں].
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ** (*Allahu Akbar*)
  - *Allahu* = Ism (Name of Allah).
  - *Akbaru* = Ism (The Greatest).
* **Practice**: Is **مِنْ** (*Min* - from) an Ism, Fi'l, or Harf? *(Answer: Harf)*.

---

### Day 2: Spotting an Ism (The 2 Magical Signs)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: You don't need a dictionary to spot an *Ism*. Look for these 2 signs:
  1. Starts with **الـ** (*Al-* = "The"), e.g., **الْحَمْدُ** (*Al-Hamdu*).
  2. Ends with **Tanween** (double vowels: ٌ ً ٍ), e.g., **مَسْجِدٌ** (*Masjidun* = A mosque).
* **Today's 3 Words**:
  1. **الْبَيْتُ** (*Al-Baytu*) = The house (Ism - has *Al-*).
  2. **رَسُولٌ** (*Rasoolun*) = A messenger (Ism - has Tanween).
  3. **سَمِعَ** (*Sami'a*) = He heard (Fi'l - has neither!).
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **بِسْمِ اللَّهِ** (*Bismil-Laah*)
  - *Bi-* = Harf (With).
  - *Ismi* = Ism (Name).
  - *Al-Laahi* = Ism (Allah).
* **Practice**: Does an Arabic verb (*Fi'l*) ever take *Al-* or Tanween? *(Answer: Never!)*.

---

### Day 3: One, Two, and Many (واحد، تثنیہ، جمع)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: English and Urdu have Singular and Plural. Arabic has a special **Dual** for exactly TWO:
  - Singular: **مُسْلِمٌ** (*Muslimun* = 1 Muslim).
  - Dual (Add **ـَانِ / -aani**): **مُسْلِمَانِ** (*Muslimaani* = 2 Muslims).
  - Plural (Add **ـُونَ / -oona** or **ـِينَ / -eena**): **مُسْلِمُونَ** (*Muslimoona* = 3+ Muslims).
* **Today's 3 Words**:
  1. **مُؤْمِنٌ** (*Mu'minun*) = 1 Believer.
  2. **مُؤْمِنَانِ** (*Mu'minaani*) = 2 Believers.
  3. **مُؤْمِنُونَ** (*Mu'minoona*) = Many Believers.
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **الْعَالَمِينَ** (*Al-'Aalameen*) in Surah Al-Fatiha
  - Singular is *'Aalam* (عالم = world).
  - Ending **ـِينَ** (*-eena*) makes it plural $\rightarrow$ "All the worlds / All creations".

---

### Day 4: Standalone Pronouns (I, You, He, She, We, They)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: Standalone pronouns are *Ism* and stand by themselves as subjects:
  - **هُوَ** (*Huwa*) = He (وہ ایک مرد).
  - **هِيَ** (*Hiya*) = She (وہ ایک عورت).
  - **هُمْ** (*Hum*) = They all (وہ سب).
  - **أَنْتَ** (*Anta*) = You (آپ / تم).
  - **أَنَا** (*Anaa*) = I (میں).
  - **نَحْنُ** (*Nahnu*) = We (ہم).
* **Today's 3 Words**:
  1. **هُوَ** (*Huwa*) = He / It.
  2. **أَنَا** (*Anaa*) = I.
  3. **نَحْنُ** (*Nahnu*) = We.
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ** (*Surah Al-Ikhlas 112:1*)
  - *Qul* = Fi'l (Say!).
  - *Huwa* = Pronoun Ism (He is).
  - *Allahu* = Ism (Allah).
  - *Ahad* = Ism (The One and Only).

---

### Day 5: Attached Pronouns (His, Your, My, Our)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: When pronouns attach to the end of a word like a tail:
  - **ـهُ** (*-hu*) = His (اس کا). Example: *Kitabu-hu* (his book).
  - **ـكَ** (*-ka*) = Your (آپ کا). Example: *Kitabu-ka* (your book).
  - **ـي** (*-ee*) = My (میرا). Example: *Kitab-ee* (my book).
  - **ـنَا** (*-naa*) = Our (ہمارا). Example: *Kitabu-naa* (our book).
* **Today's 3 Words**:
  1. **رَبِّي** (*Rabbee*) = My Lord (*Rabb* + *-ee*).
  2. **رَبَّنَا** (*Rabbanaa*) = Our Lord (*Rabb* + *-naa*).
  3. **رَبُّكَ** (*Rabbuka*) = Your Lord (*Rabb* + *-ka*).
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ** (*Ruku' Tasbeeh*)
  - *Subhaana* = Flawlessly pure is.
  - *Rabbi* = My Lord (*Rabb* + *-ee*).
  - *Al-'Azeem* = The Magnificent.

---

### Day 6: The 8 Super Prepositions (Huroof al-Jarr) & The Zer Rule
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: In Arabic, prepositions (*Fee, Min, Ila, 'Ala, Bi, Li, 'An, Ka*) force the next noun to take a **Kasrah / Zer (ِ)** at the end!
  - Normal: *Al-Bayt**u*** (الْبَيْتُ - The house).
  - With Preposition: *Fee al-Bayt**i*** (فِي الْبَيْتِ - In the house).
* **Today's 3 Words**:
  1. **فِي** (*Fee*) = In [Urdu: میں].
  2. **مِنْ** (*Min*) = From [Urdu: سے].
  3. **عَلَى** (*'Alaa*) = Upon / On [Urdu: پر].
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **عَلَىٰ عِبَادِ اللَّهِ الصَّالِحِينَ** (*In Tashahhud*)
  - *'Alaa* = Harf Jarr (Upon).
  - *'Ibaad**i*** = Takes Zer because of *'Alaa*!
  - *Al-Laah**i*** = Takes Zer because of possession ("servants of Allah").

## 📅 WEEK 2: Word Combinations & Surah Al-Fatiha (Days 8 – 14)

### Day 8: Pointing Words (This & That - Asmaa al-Ishaarah)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**:
  - Near: **هَٰذَا** (*Haadhaa* = This masc.), **هَٰذِهِ** (*Haadhihi* = This fem.).
  - Far: **ذَٰلِكَ** (*Dhaalika* = That masc.), **تِلْكَ** (*Tilka* = That fem.).
* **Today's 3 Words**:
  1. **هَٰذَا** (*Haadhaa*) = This (یہ).
  2. **ذَٰلِكَ** (*Dhaalika*) = That (وہ).
  3. **تِلْكَ** (*Tilka*) = That (female/broken plural).
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **ذَٰلِكَ الْكِتَابُ لَا رَيْبَ فِيهِ** (*Surah Al-Baqarah 2:2*)
  - *Dhaalika* = That (Pointing Ism).
  - *Al-Kitaabu* = The Book (Ism).
  - *Laa rayba* = No doubt (Negation).
  - *Fee-hi* = In it (*Fee* + *-hi*).

---

### Day 9: The Possessive Duo (Murakkab Idaafi - "X of Y")
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: When two nouns join to show possession ("Book of Allah", "House of Allah"):
  1. Word 1 (The possessed): **Never** takes *Al-* and **never** takes Tanween.
  2. Word 2 (The owner): **Always** takes a **Kasrah / Zer (ِ)**!
* **Today's 3 Words**:
  1. **كِتَابُ اللَّهِ** (*Kitaabu-l-Laahi*) = Book of Allah.
  2. **بَيْتُ اللَّهِ** (*Baytu-l-Laahi*) = House of Allah (Ka'bah).
  3. **رَسُولُ اللَّهِ** (*Rasoolu-l-Laahi*) = Messenger of Allah.
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ** (*Surah Al-Fatiha 1:4*)
  - *Maaliki* = Master (Word 1 - no *Al-*, no Tanween).
  - *Yawmi* = Day of (Word 2 - takes Zer).
  - *Ad-Deen* = Judgment (takes Zer). $\rightarrow$ *"Master of the Day of Judgment"*.

---

### Day 10: The Describing Duo (Noun + Adjective - Murakkab Tawsifi)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: In Arabic, the noun comes FIRST and the describing adjective comes SECOND! They are like twins—they match in *Al-* and endings:
  - *Al-Qur'aan**u*** *Al-Kareem**u*** (The Noble Qur'an).
  - *Siraat**an*** *Mustaqeem**an*** (A straight path).
* **Today's 3 Words**:
  1. **كَبِيرٌ** (*Kabeerun*) = Big / Great.
  2. **كَرِيمٌ** (*Kareemun*) = Noble / Generous.
  3. **مُسْتَقِيمٌ** (*Mustaqeemun*) = Straight.
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ** (*Surah Al-Fatiha 1:6*)
  - *As-Siraata* = The Path (The Noun).
  - *Al-Mustaqeema* = The Straight (The Adjective matching *As-Siraat* in *Al-* and Zabar!).

---

### Day 11: The Simple Sentence (Jumlah Ismiyyah)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: Arabic does not have a word for "IS" or "ARE"! When you place a definite noun (starts with *Al-*) next to an indefinite noun (ends with Tanween), an invisible **"IS"** automatically appears between them!
  - *Allahu* (الله) + *Ghafoorun* (غفورٌ) = **Allahu Ghafoorun** $\rightarrow$ "Allah **IS** Forgiving".
* **Today's 3 Words**:
  1. **غَفُورٌ** (*Ghafoorun*) = Forgiving.
  2. **رَحِيمٌ** (*Raheemun*) = Merciful.
  3. **عَلِيمٌ** (*'Aleemun*) = All-Knowing.
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **اللَّهُ غَفُورٌ رَحِيمٌ** (*Surah Al-Baqarah 2:218*)
  - *Allahu* = The Subject (Allah).
  - *Ghafoorun Raheem* = The News / Information (IS Forgiving, Merciful).

---

### Day 12: Demystifying I'raab (Pesh, Zabar, Zer Demystified)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: Arabic short vowels at the end of words are not decorations; they tell you the job of the word:
  - **Pesh / Dammah (ُ)** = **The Boss / Subject** (Who is doing the action or what the sentence is about).
  - **Zabar / Fathah (َ)** = **The Object / Detail** (What is being acted upon).
  - **Zer / Kasrah (ِ)** = **Possession or after a Preposition** (Belonging to).
* **Visual Color Code**:
  - Blue = Pesh (Subject).
  - Red = Zabar (Object).
  - Green = Zer (Preposition / Belonging).

---

### Day 13: Sentence Shifters (Inna & Its Sisters)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: The particle **إِنَّ** (*Inna* = Surely/Indeed) adds powerful certainty to a sentence. Its grammar trick: it changes the subject's ending from Pesh (ُ) to **Zabar (َ)**!
  - Normal: *Allahu ma'a as-saabireen* (Allah is with the patient).
  - With Inna: **إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَ الصَّابِرِينَ** (*Inna-l-Laah**a** ma'as-saabireen* = Surely Allah is with the patient).
* **Today's 3 Words**:
  1. **إِنَّ** (*Inna*) = Surely / Indeed / Verily.
  2. **أَنَّ** (*Anna*) = That (used in the middle of sentences).
  3. **مَعَ** (*Ma'a*) = With.

## 📅 WEEK 3: The Verb Engine (Sarf - علم الصرف) (Days 15 – 21)

### Day 15: The 3-Letter Root Secret (علم الاشتقاق)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: 99% of Arabic words come from a 3-letter root family.
* **The Engine Example**: Root **ع - ل - م** (Knowing):
  - *'Alima* (He knew).
  - *'Allama* (He taught).
  - *'Ilm* (Knowledge).
  - *'Aalim* (Scholar / Knower).
  - *Ma'loom* (Known fact).
  - *Mu'allim* (Teacher).
* **Today's 3 Roots**:
  1. **خ - ل - ق** (*Kh-L-Q*) = Creating (*Khalaqa, Khaaliq, Makhlooq*).
  2. **ع - ب - د** (*'-B-D*) = Worshipping (*'Abada, 'Aabid, 'Ibaadah*).
  3. **ر - ح - م** (*R-H-M*) = Mercy (*Rahima, Rahmaan, Raheem*).

---

### Day 16: Past Verbs (فعل ماضی) — He Did, They Did
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: Past tense is shown by what is attached to the **end** of the 3 root letters:
  - Root: **فَعَلَ** (*Fa'ala* = He did - base form).
  - Add Waw + Alif (**ـُوا**): **فَعَلُوا** (*Fa'aloo* = They all did).
* **Today's 3 Verbs**:
  1. **خَلَقَ** (*Khalaqa* = He created) $\rightarrow$ **خَلَقُوا** (*Khalaqoo* = They created).
  2. **آمَنَ** (*Aamana* = He believed) $\rightarrow$ **آمَنُوا** (*Aamanoo* = They believed).
  3. **كَفَرَ** (*Kafara* = He disbelieved) $\rightarrow$ **كَفَرُوا** (*Kafaroo* = They disbelieved).
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ**
  - *Alladheena* = Those who.
  - *Aamanoo* = They believed (*Aamana* + *-oo*).
  - *Wa 'amiloo* = And they did righteous deeds (*'Amila* + *-oo*).

---

### Day 17: Past Verbs (فعل ماضی) — You Did, I Did, We Did
* **The 1 Simple Rule**:
  - Add **ـْتَ** (*-ta*): **فَعَلْتَ** (*Fa'alta* = You did).
  - Add **ـْتُ** (*-tu*): **فَعَلْتُ** (*Fa'altu* = I did).
  - Add **ـْنَا** (*-naa*): **فَعَلْنَا** (*Fa'alnaa* = We did).
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ** (*Surah Al-Fatiha 1:7*)
  - *An'am-**ta*** = **You** bestowed favor! (Notice the *-ta* suffix!).
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **إِنَّا أَعْطَيْنَاكَ الْكَوْثَرَ** (*Surah Al-Kawthar 108:1*)
  - *A'tay-**naa**-ka* = **We** gave **you**! (*-naa* = We; *-ka* = You).

---

### Day 18: Present & Future Verbs (فعل مضارع) — The Prefix Code
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: While past verbs take endings, present verbs take **prefixes**! Remember the code word **انِیتَ** (*Anee-tu*):
  - **أَ** (*A-*) = I do (e.g., *Ash-hadu* = I bear witness).
  - **نَـ** (*Na-*) = We do (e.g., *Na'budu* = We worship).
  - **يَـ** (*Ya-*) = He/They do (e.g., *Ya'lamu* = He knows).
  - **تَـ** (*Ta-*) = You/She does (e.g., *Ta'lamu* = You know).
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ**
  - *Na-*-'budu = **We** worship (Starts with *Na-*).
  - *Na-*-sta'een = **We** seek help (Starts with *Na-*).

---

### Day 19: Commands & Duas (فعل أمر ونہی)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**:
  - Command to do: usually starts with Alif and ends in a Sukun: **اِفْعَلْ** (*If'al* = Do!), **اِعْلَمْ** (*I'lam* = Know!).
  - When we ask Allah, it's grammatically a command form, but spiritually a humble plea (*Du'a*): **اهْدِنَا** (*Ihdinaa* = Guide us!).
  - Prohibition (Don't do!): Put **لَا** (*Laa*) before the present verb with a Sukun: **لَا تَفْعَلْ** (*Laa taf'al* = Do not do!).
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ** (*Surah Al-Fatiha 1:6*)
  - *Ihdi* = Guide! (Command verb).
  - *-naa* = Us.

---

### Day 20: The Doer vs The Receiver (Ism Faa'il & Ism Maf'ool)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**:
  - Pattern **فَاعِل** (*Faa'il*) = The Person Doing:
    - *Khaalaqa* $\rightarrow$ **خَالِقٌ** (*Khaaliq* = Creator).
    - *'Abada* $\rightarrow$ **عَابِدٌ** (*'Aabid* = Worshipper).
    - *Sabara* $\rightarrow$ **صَابِرٌ** (*Saabir* = Patient one).
  - Pattern **مَفْعُول** (*Maf'ool*) = The Object Done Unto:
    - *Khalaqa* $\rightarrow$ **مَخْلُوقٌ** (*Makhlooq* = Created being).
    - *Rahima* $\rightarrow$ **مَرْحُومٌ** (*Marhoom* = Received mercy).
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ** (*Surah Al-Fatiha 1:7*)
  - *Al-Maghdoob* = On the pattern of *Maf'ool* $\rightarrow$ "Those upon whom anger/wrath was poured".

## 📅 WEEK 4: Quranic Patterns, Questions & Hadith (Days 22 – 28)

### Day 22: Negation Tools (Saying "No" in Arabic)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**:
  - **مَا** (*Maa*) = Did not (negates past): *Maa fa'ala* (He did not do).
  - **لَا** (*Laa*) = Does not / Not (general/present): *Laa ya'lam* (He does not know).
  - **لَمْ** (*Lam*) = Did not (firm past): *Lam yalid* (He did not beget).
  - **لَنْ** (*Lan*) = Will NEVER (strong future): *Lan tanaaloo* (You shall never attain).
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ** (*Surah Al-Ikhlas 112:3*)
  - *Lam yalid* = He did not beget.
  - *Wa lam yoolad* = And was not begotten.

---

### Day 23: Question Tools (أسماء الاستفهام)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**:
  - **مَنْ** (*Man*) = Who?
  - **مَا / مَاذَا** (*Maa / Maadhaa*) = What?
  - **كَيْفَ** (*Kayfa*) = How?
  - **أَيْنَ** (*Ayna*) = Where?
  - **مَتَى** (*Mataa*) = When?
  - **هَلْ / أَ** (*Hal / A-*) = Is it? / Did you?
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصْحَابِ الْفِيلِ** (*Surah Al-Fil 105:1*)
  - *A-* = Question mark (Did?).
  - *Lam tara* = You not see?
  - *Kayfa* = How?
  - *Fa'ala Rabbuka* = Your Lord dealt...

---

### Day 24: Common Irregular Roots (Weak Verbs)
* **The 1 Simple Rule**: When a root has Waw (و) or Ya (ي), they often turn into an Alif (ا) in past tense:
  - Root *Q-W-L* $\rightarrow$ **قَالَ** (*Qaala* = He said) $\rightarrow$ Present: **يَقُولُ** (*Yaqoolu* = He says) $\rightarrow$ Command: **قُلْ** (*Qul* = Say!).
  - Root *K-W-N* $\rightarrow$ **كَانَ** (*Kaana* = He was) $\rightarrow$ Present: **يَكُونُ** (*Yakoonu* = He is).
* **Live Scripture Breakdown**: **إِذَا جَاءَ نَصْرُ اللَّهِ وَالْفَتْحُ** (*Surah An-Nasr 110:1*)
  - *Idhaa* = When.
  - *Jaa'a* = Comes (*J-Y-'*).
  - *Nasru-l-Laah* = The help of Allah.

---

### Day 28: First Authentic Hadith Analysis
* **The Mission**: Imam Nawawi's Hadith 1:
  > **إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ ، وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى**
  - *Innamaa* = Only / Actions are exclusively judged.
  - *Al-A'maalu* = Actions (Plural of *'Amal* - Ism).
  - *Bi-* = By / According to (Harf Jarr).
  - *An-Niyyaat* = Intentions (Plural of *Niyyah* - takes Zer!).
  - *Wa innamaa* = And only.
  - *Li-kulli imri'in* = For every person.
  - *Maa nawaa* = Is what he intended (*Nawaa* = Past verb).

### Day 29: Reading Unvowelled Text Basics
* **The Secret**: How to read without Zabar/Zer/Pesh:
  1. If it starts a sentence, give it a **Pesh** (ُ).
  2. If it follows *In*, *From*, *To*, *On*, give it a **Zer** (ِ).
  3. If it's what the verb is doing something to, give it a **Zabar** (َ).
* **Practice**: Reading 3 simple vowelless sentences and adding the correct Harakat.
