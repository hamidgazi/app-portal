# 🕌 Daily Salah (Namaz) Linguistic Anatomy Cheatsheet
*Project: Classical & Quranic Arabic for Islamic Texts (20-Arabic-Mastery)*

Transform your 5 daily prayers from ritual recitation into conscious conversation with Allah ﷻ.

---

## 1. Entering Prayer: Takbeer-e-Tahreema (تکبیر تحریمہ)
> **اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ** (*Allahu Akbar*)
- **اللَّهُ**: The divine name, ending in Dammah / Pesh (ُ) because He is the Subject / Focus.
- **أَكْبَرُ**: Pattern *Af'alu* (أفعل) = Superlative. Meaning: Greater than your job, family, stress, phone, worries, and all creation.

---

## 2. Opening Supplication: Thana / Istiftaah (ثناء)
> **سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ وَتَعَالَىٰ جَدُّكَ وَلَا إِلَٰهَ غَيْرُكَ**
| Arabic Token | Grammatical Breakdown | English Meaning | Urdu Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **سُبْحَانَـ** | Verbal noun of purity (*Tanzih*) | Completely free from defect | بے عیب اور پاک |
| **ـكَ** | Attached 2nd person pronoun | You / Your | آپ |
| **اللَّهُمَّ** | Vocative particle substitution | O Allah! | اے اللہ! |
| **وَ** | Conjunction | And | اور |
| **بِـ** | Preposition | With / By | ساتھ |
| **حَمْدِكَ** | *Hamd* (Praise) + *-ka* (Your) | Your praise | آپ کی تعریف |
| **تَبَارَكَ** | Past tense verb (*Tafa'ala*) | Full of blessing is | برکت والا ہے |
| **اسْمُكَ** | *Ism* (Name) + *-ka* (Your) | Your Name | آپ کا نام |
| **تَعَالَىٰ** | Past tense verb (*Tafa'ala*) | Supremely Exalted is | بلند و برتر ہے |
| **جَدُّكَ** | *Jadd* (Majesty) + *-ka* (Your) | Your Majesty & Glory | آپ کی شان و عظمت |
| **وَلَا** | And + Negative | And no | اور نہیں |
| **إِلَٰهَ** | Noun of generic negation | Deity worthy of worship | کوئی معبود |
| **غَيْرُكَ** | *Ghayr* (Other) + *-ka* (You) | Other than You | آپ کے سوا |

## 3. Ruku' & Standing (الركوع والقيام)

### In Ruku' (رکوع کی تسبیح):
> **سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ** (*Subhaana Rabbi-ya-l-'Azeem*)
- **سُبْحَانَ**: Flawlessly pure and free of imperfection.
- **رَبِّـ**: Nourisher, Sustainer, Master (پالنے والا).
- **ـيَ**: Attached 1st person pronoun = **My** (میرا). $\rightarrow$ *"My personal Sustainer"*.
- **الْعَظِيمِ**: The Magnificent, Supreme, possessing all majesty.

### Rising from Ruku' (تسمیع و تحمید):
> **سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ — رَبَّنَا لَكَ الْحَمْدُ**
- **سَمِعَ**: Past verb = Heard and answered favorably.
- **اللَّهُ**: The Subject (Allah).
- **لِـ**: For / Towards.
- **مَنْ**: The one who / Whoever.
- **حَمِدَ**: Praised.
- **ـهُ**: Him.
- **رَبَّنَا**: *Rabb* + *-naa* = **Our Lord!**
- **لَكَ**: *Li* + *-ka* = **To You alone belongs** (exclusive dedication).
- **الْحَمْدُ**: All gratitude, recognition, and supreme praise.

---

## 4. Sujood (السجود)
> **سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَىٰ** (*Subhaana Rabbi-ya-l-A'laa*)
- **سُبْحَانَ**: Flawless purity.
- **رَبِّيَ**: My personal Sustainer and Master.
- **الْأَعْلَىٰ**: The Most High. While your forehead is on the lowest possible physical point on earth (dust), your heart proclaims Allah at the highest supreme station.

---

## 5. Between the Two Sujood (بین السجدتین)
> **رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي ، رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي** (*Rabbi-ghfir lee, Rabbi-ghfir lee*)
- **رَبِّ**: My Lord!
- **اغْفِرْ**: Command/Prayer verb from root *Gh-F-R* = Forgive and shield!
- **لِي**: *Li* (for) + *-ee* (me) = For me!

---

## 6. Tashahhud (التحيات)
> **التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ**
> **السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ**
> **السَّلَامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَىٰ عِبَادِ اللَّهِ الصَّالِحِينَ**
> **أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ**

- **التَّحِيَّاتُ**: All oral praise, greetings of royalty, and verbal venerations.
- **الصَّلَوَاتُ**: All physical bodily acts of worship, bowings, and prostrations.
- **الطَّيِّبَاتُ**: All pure spending, charities, and beautiful deeds.
- **السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ**: Peace and total divine security be upon you (*'Alaa* + *-ka*).
- **عَلَيْنَا**: Upon us (*'Alaa* + *-naa*).
- **عِبَادِ اللَّهِ**: The servants of Allah (*'Ibaad* = plural of *'Abd*).
- **الصَّالِحِينَ**: The righteous and virtuous ones.
- **أَشْهَدُ**: I testify (*A-* = I; *Shahida* = to witness).
- **عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ**: His dedicated slave/servant and His designated Messenger.
