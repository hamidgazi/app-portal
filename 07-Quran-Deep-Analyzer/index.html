🎨 2. Design Architecture & Ayah Stop Medallions

Why were there 2 big circles previously?

In previous iterations, the Ayah stop marker suffered from a distracting double-circle visual defect:

  1. Circle 1: The outer CSS container .quran-ayah-stop-mark was styled as a circle with a border and heavy neon glow.
  2. Circle 2: Inside that container, the character ۝ (U+06DD End of Ayah) was rendered. In Arabic typography, ۝ is already an ornate circular rosette glyph!
  3. The Result: The browser drew the CSS border circle around the font's inner rosette circle, creating two visible concentric rings like an archery target, ballooning to an oversized 2.15em (>60px tall) that overwhelmed the sacred verse text.
✨ The Redesigned Ayah Stop Medallion:
  • Single Delicate Ring: The duplicate font rosette glyph is cleanly suppressed, eliminating concentric rings.
  • Proportional Scale (1.28em): Scaled down by over 40% to sit harmoniously on the baseline alongside text.
  • Antique Gold Hairline: Styled with 1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45) and subtle translucent tint, restoring sacred text focus.
  • Interactive Waqf Popover: Click any medallion to display the instant trilingual phonetic pause rule (Urdu, Roman Urdu, English).
